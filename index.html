Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch or upgrade my streaming tier? ▼
You can upgrade or downgrade anytime via the #account page. Upgrades take effect immediately so you can enjoy 4K UHD and spatial audio right away.
How does offline downloading work? ▼
Click the 'Download' button on any movie or series to store an encrypted offline copy on your device. Once downloaded, you can watch anytime on the #downloads page without using mobile data.
Can I watch simultaneously with friends remotely? ▼
Yes! Our built-in Watch Party mode synchronizes playback across all participants and includes real-time live chat and interactive reaction bursts directly on your screen.
What are the requirements for 4K Ultra HD playback? ▼
To stream in 4K UHD, you need: A Premium Netflix plan, a 60Hz 4K-capable display, and a steady internet connection speed of 25 Mbps or higher.
